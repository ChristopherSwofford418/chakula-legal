Privacy Policy

This policy explains how ClearPass Technologies handles information when you use Chakula: Calorie & Macro (“Chakula”).

Chakula is a local-first, general-wellness food journal for adults. It does not require an account or cloud sync to use its core features.

Information stored on your device

Chakula stores your food library, food entries, saved meals, reflections, personal references, display choices, and reminder settings in the app’s local database on your device. This information is used to provide the journal features you choose. It is not uploaded to ClearPass Technologies by default.

Optional pseudonymous product analytics

Product analytics is off by default. If you choose “Help improve Chakula” in Data & Privacy and the app is configured for analytics, Chakula sends a limited set of product-interaction events to Mixpanel to understand how app features are used. These may include opening the paywall, creating a journal entry, selecting a display mode, or starting an export. We do not send purchase-completion outcomes, Apple receipts, your food names, brands, journal notes, calorie or macro values, personal references, reflection text, support messages, or other free-text journal content as our chosen event fields.

Mixpanel’s mobile SDK may attach a pseudonymous, app-generated device identifier, device and app metadata, and the event time. That identifier can link your opted-in product interactions across app sessions. Although Chakula configures the SDK to disable IP-derived geolocation, the processor receives network requests and may process coarse location if that setting is ineffective; no precise location permission is requested. We do not use these events for cross-app tracking or targeted advertising.

Turning analytics off prevents future Chakula analytics events and resets the local SDK identifier. It does not automatically delete events previously received by Mixpanel. Contact us through the existing support or privacy channel if you would like help with a provider-side request, subject to what the processor can identify and erase.

Purchases

Chakula Plus purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. ClearPass Technologies does not receive or store your payment card number. Apple may process purchase and subscription information under Apple’s privacy policy.

Exports and deletion

You can export your local journal as JSON or CSV from within the app without subscribing. An optional Plus weekly recap CSV contains only aggregated day and meal-type counts and opens the device’s share sheet at your request. You can also delete local Chakula data from Data & Privacy. Deletion removes the local food library, saved meals, entries, references, reflections, reminder preferences, and local analytics choice from this device; starter foods are recreated for a fresh start. Local deletion does not erase any analytics events already received by Mixpanel, Apple subscription records, copies you exported or shared, or device backups.

Support requests

If you email us for support, we use the information you provide in your message to respond and troubleshoot. Please do not include sensitive health, payment, or account information in an email.

Children

Chakula is intended for adults and is not directed to children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The effective date above indicates when this version took effect.
