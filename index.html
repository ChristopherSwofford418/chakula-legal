Support, privacy, and terms

Food logging, your way.

Chakula is a private, Tanzania-first food journal for adults. It helps you log familiar meals, build your own food library, and choose whether to view nutrition values.

Need help?

For help with Chakula, privacy requests, or feedback, contact the support team by email. We aim to reply with clear, practical help.

Email Chakula Support

Your privacy at a glance

Your food library, journal entries, notes, reflections, references, and reminders stay on your device by default. Chakula does not require an account or cloud sync.

Optional anonymous analytics is off by default. It is designed not to include food names, nutrition values, notes, reflections, personal references, dates, locations, or contact information.

Read the Chakula Privacy Policy

General-wellness boundary

Chakula is a general-wellness journal. It does not provide medical advice, diagnoses, treatment, meal plans, or dietary targets. If you need medical or nutrition guidance, contact a qualified professional.
