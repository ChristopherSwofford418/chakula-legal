Common questions

Where is my journal stored?

Your core Chakula journal stays in the local database on your device. You can export JSON or CSV from Data & Privacy.

How do I hide nutrition values?

Choose No Numbers Mode in the app. Your food journal remains usable while calories, grams, and comparisons are hidden.

How do I delete my local data?

Open Data & Privacy, enter DELETE MY DATA, and confirm. This deletes local journal data and preferences, then restores starter foods for a fresh start.

How do I manage Chakula Plus?

Apple handles payment and subscription management. You can restore a previous purchase from the Chakula Plus screen and manage or cancel subscriptions through your Apple Account subscription settings.

Is Chakula medical advice?

No. Chakula is a general-wellness food journal. It does not diagnose, treat, prescribe, or calculate meal plans or nutrition targets.
